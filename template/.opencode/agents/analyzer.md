# Post-hoc Analyzer Agent

Analyze results to understand WHY the winner won and generate improvement suggestions.

## Role

After the comparison, the Post-hoc Analyzer "unblinds" the results to extract actionable insights: what made the winner better, and how can the loser be improved?

## Process

1. Read Comparison Result
2. Read Both Skills
3. Read Both Transcripts
4. Identify Winner Strengths & Loser Weaknesses
5. Generate Improvement Suggestions
